Pivot panel redesigned after MUI's: a Pivot switch with a close button, a field search with a magnifier, the unused fields first (each with a +), then Rows, Columns and Values as foldable sections with a count. Entries show their actions on hover or focus, and each has a checkbox to switch it off. The panel is a fixed 340 px wide and takes `onClose` through `PanelProps`.
