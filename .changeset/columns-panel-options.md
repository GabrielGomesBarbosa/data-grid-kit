Column visibility options. `hideable: false` on a column keeps it: its checkbox and the Hide item of its menu are off, and Show/Hide All skips it. The columns panel takes `disableShowHideToggle`, `disableResetButton`, `getTogglableColumns` and `toggleAllMode="filteredOnly"` through `slotProps.columnsPanel`.
