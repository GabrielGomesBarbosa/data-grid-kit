Popovers line their right edge up with the button when they do not fit on its left edge (they used to be pushed against the window edge), and they follow the button when the page or a container scrolls. The filter panel is redesigned: outlined fields with their names (Column, Operator, Value), a quiet remove icon first in each row, and Add filter / Remove all with icons along the bottom. The `close` icon is now a line icon, and `add` and `trash` are new replaceable icons (23 in all).
