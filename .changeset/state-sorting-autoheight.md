---
'data-grid-kit': minor
---

`api.exportState()` and `api.restoreState(state)` save and bring back the arrangement of the grid (sorting, filters, page, column order, widths, visibility and pinning, grouping, aggregation, pivot, density, expansion, pinned rows) as plain data. `sortingOrder` (grid or column) sets the turns of a click on a header, and `multipleColumnsSortingMode="always"` adds to the sort without a modifier key (Ctrl and Cmd now work like Shift). `autoHeight` sizes the grid to its rows.
