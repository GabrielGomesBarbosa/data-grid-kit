# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

**Our standard.** Be respectful and constructive. Welcome newcomers, assume good intent, give feedback on the work and not the person, and accept feedback the same way.

**Unacceptable behaviour** includes harassment or discrimination of any kind, insults or personal attacks, publishing someone's private information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

**Enforcement.** Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor for behaviour they judge inappropriate. To report a problem, open a private security advisory or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.

**Scope.** This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in public.
